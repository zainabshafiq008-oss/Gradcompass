02 · WHY EXISTING TOOLS FAIL

"Existing tools give you data. None of them give you direction."

✗

University rankings ignore visa reality. They tell you Oxford is #1 — they don't tell you that some passports face elevated refusal rates.

✗

Job boards don't know your degree. A Psychology grad sees "Nurse" because the country hires nurses. No personalization.

✗

✗

No tool projects by graduation year. A 2026 grad and a 2030 grad see the same rankings, even though the labour market shifts dramatically.

04 · SIX KEY INNOVATIONS

"Six things no other career tool does."

1

Passport-Aware Visa Scoring

Your nationality adjusts every visa score. A Pakistani student sees different options than a Canadian.

2

Degree-Aware Job Matching

Forensic Science majors see forensic jobs. Computer Science majors see engineering jobs. No cross-contamination.

3

University-Aware Scoring

Top-tier universities unlock visa fast-tracks (UK HPI, Dutch Orientation Year, UAE Golden Visa) and get more recruiter attention.

4

Graduation-Year Forecasts

Recommendations shift based on when you graduate — near-term vs long-term market windows. A 2030 grad sees UAE and Saudi climb.

5

Licensed Profession Warnings

Medicine, law, and nursing students see realistic licensing timelines before they apply.

6

Language & IELTS/TOEFL Requirements

Every country shows exactly what language tests you need.
